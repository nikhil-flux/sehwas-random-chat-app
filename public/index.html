Safety first

Sehwas is for adults only (18+). Video and audio travel directly between you and the other person and are never recorded by our server. Nudity, harassment and illegal content are not allowed. Use the Report button and repeat offenders are blocked automatically. Never share personal information with strangers.

FAQ

What is Sehwas?

Sehwas is a free site that connects you by video with a random person, like a digital chance meeting.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for.

Does it cost anything?

No, Sehwas is free.

Why can't I see the other person?
