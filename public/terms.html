Who can use Sehwas

You must be at least 18 years old. If you are under 18, do not use this site.

Rules

  • No nudity or sexual content.
  • No harassment, hate speech, threats or bullying.
  • No illegal content, and no recording or sharing another person's video without their consent.
  • No spam, advertising or impersonation.

Use the Report button to report a person. Users reported several times are blocked temporarily. We may block anyone who breaks these rules.

Privacy

  • Video and audio are sent directly between you and the other person (peer-to-peer). Our server does not record or store them.
  • We do not ask for your name, email or any account.
  • Text messages are passed through our server only to deliver them and are not stored.
  • To prevent abuse, your IP address is kept in server memory for a short time for report counting and temporary blocks. It is not stored permanently.
  • Because connections are peer-to-peer, the other person's browser can see your IP address, as with any video call. A VPN can help.

Stay safe

Never share your address, phone number, passwords or financial details with someone you meet here. If something makes you uncomfortable, press Next or Stop. If you believe someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services.
